# QuoteRecover SG

## Positioning

**Managed lead recovery for Singapore renovation and home-service businesses.**

We respond to new enquiries, qualify jobs, schedule the next step, follow up on quotes, and show owners where revenue is being lost.

## Ideal customer

- Renovation/interior-design firms
- Air-conditioning companies
- Cleaning, moving, pest-control, plumbing, and repair businesses
- 1–20 staff
- Receives leads through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, phone, or web forms
- Quotes jobs above S$500

## Delivery SOP

1. Ask for the last 30 days of enquiries/quotes, with sensitive information removed.
2. Record source, date, service, quote value, current status, and next action.
3. Create approved FAQ and pricing boundaries.
4. Draft three follow-up messages: helpful reminder, objection check, final close-the-loop.
5. Never promise price, availability, or technical claims without owner approval.
6. Escalate complaints, sensitive personal data, refunds, legal/medical questions, and unusual requests.
7. Report enquiries, replies, appointments, quotes followed up, recovered opportunities, and estimated value.

## Competitive adjustment

Do not market this as a chatbot or CRM. Competitors such as SleekFlow, respond.io, WATI, HubSpot, Kommo, and Zoho sell tools that still require setup and daily management. QuoteRecover sells the managed outcome: fewer lost leads.
