# TenderBrief SG

## Positioning

A focused tender-monitoring service for one Singapore industry, starting manually.

## Recommended first niche

Choose one of:
- IT/cybersecurity vendors
- Cleaning/facilities companies
- Event/media agencies
- Training providers

Do not cover every GeBIZ category at launch.

## Product

Each alert contains:
- Buyer and tender title
- Closing date
- Estimated fit: high/medium/low
- Required capabilities
- Key deliverables
- Questions to ask before bidding
- Bid/no-bid recommendation
- Link to the official tender

## Pricing

- Free weekly digest: top 3 opportunities
- Pro: S$29/month for filtered alerts
- Briefing: S$99/month with plain-English summaries
- Custom bid brief: S$250–500 each

## Landing-page copy

### Headline
Stop searching GeBIZ. Get only the tenders worth your time.

### Subheadline
TenderBrief filters Singapore government opportunities for [industry], summarizes the requirements, and tells you what to do next.

### CTA
Join the free weekly digest.

## Outreach message

Hi [Name] — I’m testing a focused tender alert for Singapore [industry] companies. It removes irrelevant GeBIZ listings and sends a short bid/no-bid summary with the actual deadline. I’m looking for five firms to test it free for two weeks. Would this be useful for [Company]?

## Operating rule

Use the official GeBIZ page as the source of truth. Never imply that a summary is an official interpretation, guarantee eligibility, or guarantee a win. Verify every closing date and link before sending.
