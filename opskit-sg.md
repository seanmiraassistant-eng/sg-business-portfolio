# OpsKit SG

## Positioning

Simple operating systems for small Singapore businesses that have outgrown WhatsApp, scattered spreadsheets, and memory.

## Initial packages

### Tuition Centre OpsKit
- Enquiry tracker
- Trial-class pipeline
- Parent follow-up scripts
- Attendance and renewal dashboard

### Contractor OpsKit
- Lead and quote tracker
- Site-visit checklist
- Quote follow-up board
- Job-margin calculator

### Service Business OpsKit
- Booking tracker
- Customer-status board
- Review-request scripts
- Weekly revenue report

## Pricing

- Template pack: S$49–99
- Setup and customization: S$199–500
- Monthly reporting/maintenance: S$99–299
