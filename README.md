# Sean's Low-Capital Business Portfolio

Built for fast validation in Singapore with near-zero upfront spend.

## Portfolio

### 1. QuoteRecover SG — recommended first launch
Managed WhatsApp/email quote follow-up for renovation and home-service businesses.

**Promise:** Every enquiry gets a response, every quote gets followed up, and owners see which leads are recoverable.

**Price:** S$300–800/month + optional S$300–1,000 setup.

### 2. TenderBrief SG
A filtered, plain-English tender alert and bid/no-bid briefing for one narrow industry.

**Promise:** Only see tenders relevant to your business, with the deadline and next action made clear.

**Price:** S$19–99/month initially; higher-priced custom briefs later.

### 3. OpsKit SG
Done-for-you operating templates and lightweight dashboards for one SME niche.

**Promise:** Turn scattered spreadsheets and repeated admin into one simple operating system.

**Price:** S$99–500 setup; S$49–199/month for maintenance/reporting.

## Launch order

1. QuoteRecover SG — fastest route to paid customers and strongest measurable ROI.
2. TenderBrief SG — validate as a manual subscription with a small list.
3. OpsKit SG — use insights from the first two businesses to package repeatable assets.

## Shared rule

Do not build software before getting three paying customers or ten paying subscribers. Deliver manually with Google Sheets, Gmail, WhatsApp Business, Google Calendar, and AI assistance.
